04 — Ideas

A few thoughts

Analytics

"Most dashboards answer questions nobody asked."

The hardest part of analytics isn't building the dashboard — it's understanding which decisions the business actually needs to make, and then designing backward from there.

On BI & reporting

SaaS Metrics

"Churn is a symptom, not a problem."

When churn goes up, the instinct is to fix retention. But churn usually reveals something upstream — onboarding, ICP fit, feature gaps. The number tells you where to look, not what to fix.

On growth metrics

Tracking

"Bad data is worse than no data — it gives you false confidence."

I've seen companies make major product decisions based on misconfigured events. GTM implementation quality is underrated. QA your tracking like you QA your code.

On data quality

Career

"Economics teaches you to ask: compared to what?"

Every metric only makes sense in context. A 3% conversion rate can be excellent or terrible depending on the industry, channel, and audience. Always ask the counterfactual.
